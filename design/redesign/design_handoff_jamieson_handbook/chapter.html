Analytical LC-MS

LiveUpdated 2 days ago~6 min read

Routine LC-MS is the fastest way to check reaction progress and confirm mass — this covers startup, running a sample, and shutdown for the shared analytical instrument.

Summary

Summary
Startup takes ~15 min. Always check the mobile phase reservoirs before a run — running dry mid-batch is the most common way to lose an afternoon.

Starting up

Wake the instrument from standby using the console PC, then confirm both pump channels are primed. Check the reservoir levels — A: H₂O + 0.1% formic and B: MeCN + 0.1% formic — before queuing anything.

  1. Log into the instrument PC with your group account.
  2. Open the acquisition software and confirm the source is at operating temperature.
  3. Run a blank injection to confirm baseline stability.
Safety
Never open the source enclosure while the instrument is under vacuum. Wait for the interlock to clear.

Running a sample

Dilute your sample to roughly 0.1–0.5 mg/mL in the run solvent before injecting — over-concentrated samples foul the source faster than anything else on this instrument.

Method: LCMS_generic_5min.m
Injection volume: 2 µL
Gradient: 5→95% B over 3.5 min, hold 1 min
How to
If the mass doesn't match, check the ±0.3 min retention window first — co-eluting peaks are the usual culprit before you assume the reaction failed.
